## Headline

- **Burner 11/12 vs GuardDog 10/12 detected; false alarms 1/11 vs 1/11.**
- Burner verdict MALICIOUS on 9/12; named the exact stolen fake secret on 7/12.
- GuardDog counting *any* rule hit (incl. its informational `capability-*` rules): 12/12 detected, 8/11 false alarms.
- Median Burner scan time 90.7s (max 95.8s) per repo; GuardDog median 0.81s.

Detected = Burner verdict MALICIOUS or SUSPICIOUS. GuardDog flagged = at least one `threat-*` rule / risk (GuardDog 3.x `capability-*` rules only describe what code *can* do and fire on almost every repo).
